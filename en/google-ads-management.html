How Does It Work?

Within the daily budget, your ads go live for your selected locations and keywords. When a click or call occurs, the determined fee is deducted from your budget. Once the budget is exhausted, the ads stop and restart at the scheduled time the following day.

01

Strategy & Setup

The target audience, region, budget, and keywords are identified. The campaign structure is set up.

02

Published

Ads are displayed during specified time slots and in selected regions.

03

Tracking & Measurement

Every click, phone call, and conversion is recorded. Data is monitored in real time.

04

Continuous Optimization

Keywords, bids, and ad copies are regularly updated based on data.
