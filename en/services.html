You Likely Have One of These Issues Right Now

Most sites appear to have analytics "set up" — but the measured data is wrong or incomplete. Here's what's happening.

Form submissions, purchases, or phone clicks either don't appear in GA4 at all or are double-counted. This feeds ad optimization algorithms with wrong data, causing your budget to be endlessly wasted on the wrong audience. Tag conflicts, wrong triggers, or missing conversion marking are usually to blame.

If you don't know where your visitors come from, you can't know which channel works. Incorrect use of UTM parameters, lack of cross-domain tracking, and empty referral exclusion lists are the main causes of this problem. Traffic from unknown sources means wasted ad budget.

Google Ads shows 50 conversions, GA4 shows 20 — which is correct? Attribution model differences, ITP restrictions (1st-party cookie limit in Safari), and pixel conflicts are the source of this discrepancy. With Server-Side Tagging, data loss can be reduced by up to 80%.

If GA4 shows zero-second sessions, a 95% bounce rate, or unreasonably short visit durations, the page tracking code is likely firing multiple times or events are conflicting. Without properly configured scroll depth and engagement time, these metrics are misleading.

When consent management (Consent Mode v2) is not properly configured, you face both GDPR/Privacy Policy enforcement risk and significant data loss in ad platforms. Google Consent Mode v2 integration is the most effective way to balance data collection with privacy compliance.

Security vulnerabilities in WordPress plugins are used to compromise hundreds of sites every day. An outdated plugin is hackers' first target for opening a backdoor. Keeping it updated blocks 90% of these attacks — but updating without breaking the site also requires expertise.

85% of visitors who see a "This site is not secure" warning leave instantly. SSL renewal seems simple but when forgotten, it seriously damages both your SEO rankings and brand trust. Our proactive monitoring tracks the certificate expiry in advance; it's renewed before it expires.

A hosting failure, a wrong update, or a hack — and the site is completely gone. Without a current backup, years of accumulated content, product info, and customer data are lost irretrievably. Daily automated backups make this nightmare impossible.

Accumulated unnecessary plugins, unoptimized images, and a bloated database slow your site down. Google pushes slow sites back in rankings — and 53% of visitors abandon sites that take longer than 3 seconds to load. Monthly speed optimization prevents this loss.

A hacked site is automatically suspended when it threatens the hosting company's other customers. When a domain gets blacklisted, email deliveries stop too. Getting to this point makes both cleanup and reputation recovery expensive and time-consuming — prevention is far less costly.
